# Architecture rules
- Recipe variants may target several ranches with an empty table list meaning the whole ranch; resolve by crop and dated scope without changing planted snapshots. Why: imported programs stay isolated and prior demand is preserved.
- The executive panel reuses shared cost, consumption and expense allocation functions; date filters select planting/harvest cohorts and costs remain cumulative. Dirección receives read-only expense access, never result-screen access. Why: comparable denominators and no duplicated business math or new editing privileges.
- Parameter-based opening plant balances are marked on requests and charged once independently of receipts; normal receipt charges exclude them. Why: imported active lots can carry an authorized opening balance without fabricated tickets or duplicate costs.
- Keep all tables in the shared context and retire them with `Tabla.archivada`; operational filters and planting validation exclude retired tables, but historical and cost lookups retain them. Why: grouped imports must not duplicate available real tables or lose their records.
- Executive acreage separates planted in-cycle area, overdue harvest area and future planting; weekly harvest uses dated departures and frozen evaluations, while clearly labeled projections spread remaining yield goals over the 14-day harvest window. Why: planning area and estimates must never masquerade as active acreage or recorded harvest/payment.
- Operational acreage summaries and lists reuse filasSuperficieTablas/totalesSuperficieTablas, with one row per unclosed lot plus the table's free remainder; status subtotals use the unfiltered cohort and selection totals use selected statuses. Why: both views agree without counting a table's full area once per lot.
